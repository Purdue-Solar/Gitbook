# Shop Safety

1\) Complete all the required [BIDC quizzes](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1BenFS-Bc9BmZ0KDZRev6kEpkGCM66Fw9EIw6JIXfguM/edit?usp=drive_link)

2\) Once you have access, complete these quizzes [Klondike Student Agreement, BEP Quiz](https://engineering.purdue.edu/metools/quiz), and [EHS Training](https://www.purdue.edu/ehps/rem/training/hazcom/hazcomawarenew/hazcomawarenewpage.html). Also for the HSI Quiz or EHS Quiz (same thing different name), please do the "Purdue 2024 Hazard Communication Comprehensive Training v2" and not the awareness one. To view hit "view catalogue" in the lower right corner of the dashboard, scroll until you locate the "Purdue 2024 Hazard Communication Comprehensive Training v2" quiz. Click on the quiz. It is now saved as a favorite and will be readily available in your dashboard.

Link for Klondike Sharepoint: [https://shorturl.at/g1SNa](https://shorturl.at/g1SNa).

Alternative HSI Quiz Link see which one works best for you:

* [https://sys.hsiplatform.com/en/Purdue/legacy-platform/login](https://sys.hsiplatform.com/en/Purdue/legacy-platform/login)
* [https://otis.osmanager4.com/#/saml/login/255930](https://otis.osmanager4.com/#/saml/login/255930)

Note: The Quizzes and safety sessions expire once a year so you will have to renew them once a year. If you are a new member and are doing the HSI quiz for the first time you will probably have to self register and it should be pretty self explanatory. If not contact the Safety officer on discord

3\) You can find safety info for Klondike in the Share Point. Before doing activities please look at the Share Point and search for the respective SOPs. Make sure to navigate the PSR folder which should contain both the global SOPs and PSR specific SOPs&#x20;

4\) Reach out to the Shop and Safety Director (Spandan Mandal) to get scheduled for the next Klondike safety info session. After you have attended then only are you clear to work and come to Klondike. Please note the Shop and Safety Officer will check for the 3 Klondike Quizzes.
