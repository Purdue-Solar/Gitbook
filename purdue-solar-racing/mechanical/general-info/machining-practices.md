## CNC Machining

* physical description of how machining works
* tool like an endmill spins and cuts into a piece of metal

## Design for Manufacturability (DFM)

* basic limitations of machines mean you have to design your part to be easy to make, otherwise it takes a million tries and therefore more money + time
* utilize existing tool sizes, manual machining where possible, think about where jaws would be able to grip your part (need parallel sides)
