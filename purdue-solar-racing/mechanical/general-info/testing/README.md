# Testing

Mechanical's testing usually takes the form of physically turning, braking, and driving the car. This is the point where we confirm that our motion assemblies, stress simulations, calculations, and everything else manage to reflect real life accurately enough. ("Enough" is key - engineers have to make careful assumptions everywhere in order to simplify models of real life).

Our goal for the 26-27 year is to get several months of testing done in preparation for FSGP 2027.
