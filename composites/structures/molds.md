# Molds

#### Steps of Mold Creation

* Design - Blocking out panel layers to stack into general mold shape.
* Stackup - Cutting, labeling, and stacking panels.
* Machining - CNC machining excess material from block structure for final shape.
* Surfacing - Sanding, filling, and priming until desired smoothness is achieved.
