* timeline:
  * manufacturing year started late, freshmen finished onboarding then were assigned FEA for gate 4 (which shouldn't have existed)
  * after gate 4 is when general members were each assigned parts to make CAM for, with the idea that they'd keep a doc explaining it and once they'd finished both CAM and documentation they could get checked off and go make the part in Bechtel
  * Bechtel's process is getting an appointment for a consultation, where you get CAM checked and then a reservation made, and then you show up at your reservation with your (squared) stock
  * because things go wrong, like tolerances don't get hit or tool setup takes too long, if you didn't finish your part at your reservation you had to reschedule a new appointment and hopefully you stopped at a point that is resumable otherwise you need new squared stock
  * ended school year without some good wishbone joints and spares, needed to go to ME shop instead which has better machines actually
