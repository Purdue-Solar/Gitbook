# Kingpin Axis/Steering Axis

#### What is a Kingpin Axis?&#x20;

The line drawn from the drawn between the upper bearing center and lower ball joint that forms the axis of the tire’s rotation

<br>

{% columns %}
{% column width="50%" %}
#### Kingpin Axis Angle Front View:

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/unknown (22).png>)

The angle of the Kingpin Axis from a front view is called the **Kingpin Inclination (KPI)**, the kingpin inclination affects:

* The amount of camber gain during cornering. If the KPI is angled towards the car that creates positive camber gain, which creates camber thrust making cornering easier
* The size of the scrub radius. The further the projection of the KPA on the ground from the center of the tires contact patch, the larger the scrub radius.



<img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/unknown (23).png" alt="Scrub Radius" height="180" width="220">


{% endcolumn %}

{% column width="50%" %}
#### Kingpin Axis Angle Side View:

![](<../../../../.gitbook/assets/unknown (21).png>)

The angle of the Kingpin Axis from a side view is called the **Caster Angle.** Positive caster angles are angled towards the front of the vehicle whereas negative caster angles are angled towards the back of the vehicle. The caster angle affects:

* How the car rises and falls during cornering. This produces a self centering effect.
* Camber gain: A positive camber angle results in negative camber gain and vice versa.
{% endcolumn %}
