# Slip Angles

#### What is a Slip Angle?

A slip angle is the difference between the direction the tire is heading and where the vehicle actually goes&#x20;

<img src="../../../../.gitbook/assets/unknown (8).png" alt="Slip angle = alpha" height="205" width="444">

Slip angles are caused by the lateral deformation of a tire during cornering. The lateral grip from the tire's contact patch creates a lateral force that turns the tire away from its expected direction. Calculating slip angles under different forces is important for determining steering geometry characteristics, and turning radius.&#x20;

What affects slip angles?

* Turning Velocity
* Turning Radius
* Tire characteristics
* Weight Distribution (statically and during cornering)
* Tire contact patch size
