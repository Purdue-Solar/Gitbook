# Lux

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-08-23 211455.png" alt="" width="475"><figcaption><p>Lux rear suspension and motor</p></figcaption></figure>

Lux's rear suspension seemingly used a similar shock mount geometry to its front suspension, and the front suspension famously delaminated the chassis due to the large bending moment. One can assume this is why we pivoted away from the big-old-triangle shock mounting design.

Note from the picture that Lux uses the same Mitsuba hub motor as Artemis.

It may be worth digging through old design reviews and VDRs to rediscover the rear sus history.
