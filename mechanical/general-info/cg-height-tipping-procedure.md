# CG Height Tipping Procedure

The center of gravity (CG) of the car is an important data point used to calculate how the car reacts to various forces. The longitudinal and lateral dimensions of the CG (x and y) are easy to find by placing scales under each wheel and by measuring wheelbase and track width. However, the CG height must be found by lifting the rear wheel a known height off the ground and then doing a little trigonometry. This was especially necessary just after building Artemis when we found that the car was more than 40 kg heavier than we'd designed it to be (possibly due to unaccounted-for composites resin and epoxies, and electrical wiring).
