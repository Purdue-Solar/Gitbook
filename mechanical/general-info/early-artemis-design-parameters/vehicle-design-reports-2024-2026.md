# Vehicle Design Reports

Vehicle design reports are required by the race officials at certain deadlines in order to register for FSGP/ASC. They are long documents that detail each part of the car and explain how every component contributes to a safe car that follows regulations. Since we submit them in January and timelines don't always go as planned, there are portions of these documents that aren't accurate to the final cars' designs.
