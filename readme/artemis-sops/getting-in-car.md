# Getting in car

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (156).png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Getting in with topshell off

* foot in red area, just infront of rear rollcage mounts.
* Grab rollcage with both hands
* hoist yourself onto car, and place foot not in red area into cockpit.&#x20;
* Free to manuever into driver position.
* ABSOLUTLEY DO NOT PLACE FEET OR WEIGHT ANYWHERE ON AEROSHELL, ONLY CHASSIS
* DO NOT STEP ON WHEEL WHEN CAR IS ON FOAM BLOCKS (may step on wheels when loaded on floor)
